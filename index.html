How to Install

  1. Install BepInEx 5 (x64) into your The Walking Zombie 2 game folder if you haven't already.
  2. Extract BroomWeaponMod.zip directly into your main The Walking Zombie 2 folder so BroomWeaponMod.dll and the icon land inside BepInEx\plugins\BroomWeaponMod\.
  3. Launch the game, load your save, and press F8 to add the Broom to your inventory!
